## Corrections carried into implementation language

- “No tonne financed twice” becomes a claim about **registered receipt units within this protocol**, not all physical tonnes everywhere.
- “Bank payment and release are atomic” becomes separate bank receipt and atomic registry execution after a lender attestation.
- “Security continues under the bill of lading” becomes an **explicit lender-accepted external hand-off**, with legal effect outside software verification.
- “Different hash means forged ticket” becomes changed evidence requiring comparison/review.
- “Lenders require this permit” becomes a configurable requirement whose actual operative source must be confirmed.
- “A database cannot do this” becomes a comparison of shared authority and operations against a neutral portal/database option.
